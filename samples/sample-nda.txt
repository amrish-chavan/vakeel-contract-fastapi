MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 15 August 2026 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN

Vakeel Technologies Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 12th Floor, Maker Chambers, Nariman Point, Mumbai 400021, Maharashtra, India ("Disclosing Party");

AND

Northwind Analytics Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Plot 48, HITEC City, Hyderabad 500081, Telangana, India ("Receiving Party").

The Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

RECITALS

The Parties wish to explore a potential software services engagement relating to contract-intelligence tooling (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to each other in connection with the Purpose.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient"), whether in written, oral, electronic, or visual form, including without limitation business plans, customer lists, pricing, source code, models, prompts, datasets, product roadmaps, and the existence of discussions between the Parties.

1.2 "Representatives" means a Party's employees, officers, directors, professional advisers, and subcontractors who have a need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF CONFIDENTIALITY

2.1 The Recipient shall hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the Discloser, except to Representatives bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

2.2 The Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.3 The Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was already in the Recipient's possession without confidentiality obligation; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Discloser prompt written notice so that the Discloser may seek a protective order. The Recipient shall disclose only the portion legally required.

5. TERM AND SURVIVAL

5.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, and shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either Party gives sixty (60) days' prior written notice of non-renewal.

5.2 Confidentiality obligations shall survive for five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

Upon written request, or upon termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or securely destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing within fifteen (15) days. One copy may be retained solely for archival or legal-compliance purposes.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any licence, assignment, or other right in the other Party's intellectual property. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.

8. NON-SOLICITATION AND NON-COMPETE

8.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any employee of the Disclosing Party.

8.2 During the term of this Agreement and for thirty-six (36) months thereafter, the Receiving Party shall not, anywhere in India or any other country, develop, market, or provide any software, service, or product that is similar to or competitive with any product or service of the Disclosing Party.

9. INDEMNITY

The Receiving Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Disclosing Party and its directors, officers, and affiliates from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including full legal fees on an indemnity basis) arising out of or related to any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether or not the Disclosing Party was negligent.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 The Disclosing Party shall have no liability whatsoever under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, including for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages.

10.2 The Receiving Party's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited.

11. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

The Receiving Party shall not, for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, circumvent the Disclosing Party by dealing directly with any customer, vendor, or partner of the Disclosing Party that was introduced in connection with the Purpose.

12. ASSIGNMENT

The Disclosing Party may assign this Agreement without consent. The Receiving Party may not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, which may be withheld in the Disclosing Party's sole discretion.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

13.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Mumbai, Maharashtra. The Receiving Party irrevocably waives any objection to venue and any right to bring proceedings in any other forum.

13.3 Notwithstanding clause 13.2, the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

14. GENERAL

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and writings.

14.2 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.3 If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

14.4 Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of that provision.

14.5 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by email with read receipt or by registered post to the addresses set out above.
